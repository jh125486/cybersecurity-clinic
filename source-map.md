## Notes on the adaptation

- The Canvas module containers, syllabus schedule, and lecture files use different labels in places. The curriculum follows the numbered slide files. The restaurant exercise appears under the second Canvas container and is taught with the third deck on reframing.
- Module 08 appeared empty in the module list. The next numbered container shown was Module 10, which held the final templates and a resume resource. The handbook uses the syllabus for later team check-ins, reporting, and presentations.
- The course's onboarding pages cover Canvas, university policies, accessibility, student support, and general technical resources. Club officers can use their own onboarding materials for those topics.
- The Canvas ethics quizzes and assignment records are linked or reflected through their teaching topics where available. The club exercises use the lecture prompts. Publisher readings remain linked from the [curriculum](curriculum.md#reading-and-facilitation-references).
- The public files are newly written adaptations of the instructional material and blank templates. The original Office and PDF files, grading, student work, and partner records remain with their original holders.
