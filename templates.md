## Client memorandum

**To:** [approved recipients]  
**From:** [clinic team]  
**Date:** [date]  
**Subject:** [engagement and scope]

1. **Executive summary.** In one or two paragraphs, describe the engagement, the highest-priority finding, and the actions leadership can take first.
2. **Organization and scope.** Explain the mission, business workflows, technical setting, constraints, dates, and assessment limitations.
3. **Threats and risks.** Describe the conditions that matter to this organization and their operational consequences. Indicate what the team verified.
4. **Baseline assessment.** Cover identity and access, devices, data protection, communications, and incident readiness as applicable.
5. **Recommendations.** Choose three to five actions. Give each a rationale, responsible role, timing, and expected outcome.
6. **Longer-term resilience.** Describe realistic opportunities for training, continuity, or improvements the organization can maintain.
7. **Roadmap.** Use time horizons meaningful to the client, such as 0–3, 3–6, and 6–12 months.
8. **Close.** Confirm next steps and thank the partner. Add authorized supporting evidence or references in appendices where useful.

## Final presentation

Use eight slides as a starting point for a 30-minute client briefing, including questions.

1. Engagement purpose and approved scope
2. Organization and critical workflows
3. Main threats and business impacts
4. Current controls and gaps
5. Three to five prioritized actions
6. Roadmap with owners and timelines
7. Opportunities to strengthen resilience
8. Questions and agreed first action

## Closeout record

Record the delivery date and approved recipients, the first action and its owner, any remaining questions, and the status of evidence transfer, retention, and access removal. Ask the partner which recommendation was clearest and which needs more explanation. Capture lessons for the next cohort without identifying the partner.
